Lululemon Athletica Pebbled Duffel



"If the Coach bag was about who I wanted the world to see, this Lululemon bag was about who I was trying to become for myself. It’s the bag of early morning alarms and the smell of eucalyptus towels. It lived in the trunks of cars and on the floors of locker rooms, stuffed with yoga mats and change-of-clothes. It’s less about 'fashion' and more about 'movement'—a heavy, sturdy reminder of a time when my schedule was measured in classes and personal bests. It feels like a transitional object, marking the moment my priorities shifted from the boardroom look to the 'zen' pursuit."
