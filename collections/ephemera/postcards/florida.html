"Life is a Beach" Florida Postcard



There is something so unapologetically loud about this postcard. After the quiet, misty greens of the Azores, this feels like a sudden burst of high-definition sunshine. I remember picking this up and thinking how perfectly it captures that 'vacation brain' state—where everything is bright, the water is a impossible shade of blue, and he only goal is to find a chair under a green umbrella.

I’ve kept it in such pristine condition compared to my other travel scraps because it feels like a preserved slice of summer. It doesn't have the travel-worn scars of my other mementos; instead, it feels like a promise of a future trip or a memory of a day where everything was exactly as easy as the text on the card suggests. It’s the ideal contrast to the ruggedness of the rest of my archive—a reminder that sometimes, life really is just a beach.
