Tailor Alteration Recipt (Eva)



I kept this because it’s the blueprint for a transformation. I remember the conversation with Eva—trying to explain exactly how I wanted the front to lift while leaving that dramatic train behind. There’s something so visceral about the phrase 'put the hard material'; it reminds me of the structure and the stiffness required to make the fabric behave the way I imagined.

This slip of paper is the only proof of the work that happened inside the seams. It’s a reminder that the things I wear aren't just 'bought'—they are often engineered. Every time I see the total at the bottom, I think about the investment of time and the specific vision I had for that day. It’s not just a receipt; it’s the bridge between a garment as it was and as I needed it to be.
