Holding this slip of paper, I’m immediately taken back to that Sunday night in late August. There’s something about the way it’s folded and slightly worn that reflects the energy of the crowd at Massey Hall. I remember being on the floor, the anticipation of the 'Don't Stare At The Sun' set, and that specific feeling of being part of a shared moment before the summer faded away.

Even though digital tickets are the standard now, I kept this physical copy because a screen can't capture the texture of the night. It sits in the archive not just as proof of attendance, but as a reminder of the raw, live sound echoing off those historic walls.
