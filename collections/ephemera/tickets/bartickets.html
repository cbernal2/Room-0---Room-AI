Collection of Carnival-Style "Admit One Stubs"



here is something so anonymous yet so inviting about these stubs. Unlike the Dominic Fike ticket, these don't tell me exactly where I was or what I saw, but they remind me of the act of going out. I remember the sound of the ticket roll clicking as the attendant pulled these off—that quick, sharp tear that meant I was finally inside.

I kept this specific mix because of the colors; they look like confetti from a dozen different afternoons. Some of these were definitely from local festivals, others from small-town cinemas where the floors were sticky and the popcorn was too salty. They are the small change of my social life—proof that I showed up, paid my way, and stepped into the crowd. I don't need the names of the movies or the rides to remember the feeling of anticipation that came with holding one of these in my palm."

Title Collection of Carnival-Style "Admit One Stubs"
Category Ephemera / Entertainment Records
Origin Various (notably featuring "Indiana Ticket Co." branding)
Physical Description A pile of approximately 15–20 classic small-format paper tickets. The collection features a vibrant array of colors: hot pink, magenta, pale yellow, mint green, white, and a single orange stub in the center. Each bears the bold "ADMIT ONE" block lettering and unique six-digit serial numbers.
Condition Fair to Good. Many stubs show "rough-cut" edges where they were torn from a roll. There is visible surface wear and slight softening of the paper corners, consistent with being held in a hand or pocket before entry.
