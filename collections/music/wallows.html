Nothing Happens by Wallows




This is the second record I was ever gifted.

Nothing Happens by Wallows (2019) is one of my favourite albums, and it reminds me of the COVID era. I remember the day my cousin brought this to me, I sat on the edge of my bed listening to the record, holding the cover like Chandler did with that one Lionel Richie album in FRIENDS. She laughed because I looked funny, but really, I was so happy to be able to hold one of the greatest albums (at that time) I had ever heard.

Wallows is a popular indie rock band, with lead singer/actor Dylan Minette also starring in hit shows like ‘13 Reasons Why’ (2017) and underrated horror films such as ‘Don’t Breathe.’ (2016) I am such a big fan of these guys and will continue to be-- they are on my bucket list of concerts I want to go to. My favourite song on this record is track nine-- Remember When, extremely fitting, right?

If Sgt. Pepper is the grandfather of the archive, Nothing Happens is the cool older sibling. This record feels like the end of high school and the beginning of everything else. The choice of a striped shirt for the cover art is incredibly intentional—it represents the 'uniform' of youth and the quiet moments that happen in bedrooms and basements. It’s an album that sounds best on a rainy afternoon or a late-night drive. Holding the physical record, you’re reminded that despite the title, everything is actually happening all at once. It captures a specific brand of suburban nostalgia that feels both universal and deeply personal
