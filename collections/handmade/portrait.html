Miniature Self-Portrait on Easel



There’s something surreal about painting myself. I remember sitting down to do this, trying to figure out which 'version' of me I wanted to freeze in time. I settled on this one—bundled up in my favorite green scarf with my headphones on, probably listening to a podcast or a new album. It’s my 'archivist mode' uniform.

I kept the name big and bold on the side because, in a digital world where everything feels anonymous, I wanted something that felt like an unapologetic claim of space. Even the little black cat in the corner feels like a necessary piece of the puzzle. It’s a tiny object, but it holds a lot of gravity for me; it’s the face of the person who is putting this whole archive together.
